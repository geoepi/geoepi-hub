## **1. Purpose and scope**

This Data Management Plan (DMP) describes how data exchanged among APHIS, ARS, and MSU under APHIS MTA #26-9419-0773 / ARS Agreement No. 21068 will be stored, accessed, transferred, documented, used, retained, and disposed of.

The data are authorized for modeling domestic cases of highly pathogenic avian influenza (HPAI), with the expectation of scientific publication and/or reporting using appropriately de-identified premises information.

This DMP applies to original data received under the agreement and to derived datasets that retain information subject to the agreement. It supplements, but does not replace, the Data Transfer Agreement. If this DMP conflicts with the agreement, the agreement controls.

## **2. Data covered**

Data covered by this DMP may include:

- sequencing data, sequence-analysis results, and associated accession information;
- metadata associated with sequence records, including collection date, species, and state;
- GIS data associated with domestic premises;
- outbreak biosecurity and epidemiology questionnaires;
- other information provided for HPAI modeling; and
- derived analytical datasets created from these controlled source data.

These categories correspond to those identified in the governing agreement.

Derived data remain subject to the protections in this DMP when they contain, reproduce, or permit reconstruction of controlled information.

Analytical code, documentation, model specifications, and other products that do not contain controlled information may be managed separately from the controlled data.

## **3. Formats, documentation, and provenance**

Original data will be retained in the form received when practical and should not be modified in place.

Working copies may be converted to standard formats appropriate for analysis, including common tabular, geospatial, sequence, document, or analytical-data formats.

Transformations from source data to analytical datasets should be reproducible and documented through project analytical workflows where practical.

A controlled-data inventory will be maintained separately from this DMP. At minimum, the inventory should identify:

- dataset name and description;
- source organization;
- date received;
- general data type and format;
- storage location;
- access or sensitivity classification;
- transfer status; and
- disposition status.

Detailed analytical provenance, including processing steps, transformations, and specific data versions, should be maintained within the analytical workflow or associated project documentation rather than duplicated in this DMP.

## **4. Storage, access, transfer, and sharing**

### **Storage**

Controlled data will be stored only on institutional systems appropriate for data of this nature and capable of restricting access to authorized individuals.

Following authorized transfer, MSU may maintain copies required for approved project activities.

Controlled data will not be intentionally stored in publicly accessible repositories, unrestricted file-sharing locations, or other systems that provide unauthorized access.

Copies should be limited to those necessary for approved project activities.

### **Access**

Access to controlled data will be limited to individuals within APHIS, ARS, or MSU who have a specific need for the data to perform authorized project activities.

Membership in a laboratory, research group, department, or organization alone does not establish a need for access.

Access should be removed when an individual's project responsibilities no longer require use of the controlled data.

### **Transfer**

Transfer of controlled data between ARS and MSU will occur only through a secure electronic mechanism appropriate for institutional use.

**Approved transfer mechanism:** TBD

The transfer mechanism should restrict access to intended recipients and protect the data during electronic transfer.

Controlled data should not be distributed through unsecured email attachments, publicly accessible links, or other unrestricted mechanisms.

Transfers should be documented sufficiently to identify the dataset transferred, the date of transfer, the sending organization, the receiving organization, and the authorized recipient or project contact.

### **Redistribution and external access**

Controlled data will not be redistributed outside APHIS, ARS, or MSU except as authorized under the governing agreement.

The agreement restricts further transfer and specifies how requests for access outside the authorized parties are to be handled.

### **Confidentiality and data protection**

Controlled data will be protected with reasonable care and in a manner consistent with the protection afforded to comparable institutional information.

The governing agreement prohibits unauthorized third-party disclosure and requires protection of received data with at least reasonable care.

These protections apply both to original data and to derived datasets from which controlled information could reasonably be recovered.

### **Premises-level information**

Precise domestic-premises information will be treated as controlled information.

Premises coordinates, identifiers, or other information capable of identifying individual premises will not be included in publicly released datasets, figures, maps, tables, presentations, or other products unless specifically authorized.

Products intended for broader dissemination must be reviewed to ensure that premises and other controlled information are sufficiently de-identified.

The governing agreement recognizes that de-identification may require treatment beyond county-level identification.

Appropriate protection may include removal of direct identifiers, reduction of geographic precision, spatial aggregation, masking, suppression, or other disclosure-control measures appropriate to the product being released.

### **Public products and publication**

There is no presumption that the underlying controlled data will be made publicly available.

Publicly disseminated products may include de-identified or aggregated analytical results, model outputs, figures, maps, tables, documentation, methods, and code that do not disclose controlled information.

Release of datasets or products derived from controlled data must be consistent with the governing agreement.

The agreement also establishes requirements concerning communication of results, manuscript review prior to publication, and acknowledgment of the data provider.

## **5. Retention and disposition**

Controlled data will be retained only while their possession and use remain authorized and necessary for approved project activities.

The governing agreement requires copies of data that have not been released into the public domain to be destroyed or otherwise disposed of as instructed by the provider no later than expiration or termination of the agreement.

The agreement remains in effect for five years from the date of the last signature unless amended or terminated earlier.

Final retention and disposition of controlled data will therefore follow the agreement and any applicable instructions issued under it.

## **6. Roles and review**

| Organization | Primary data-management responsibility |
|---|---|
| ARS | Maintain the project data currently in its possession, control access, maintain or coordinate the controlled-data inventory, and securely transfer authorized data to MSU. |
| MSU | Securely maintain received data, restrict access to personnel with a specific project need, and protect controlled information in analytical and research products. |
| APHIS | Exercise the authorities, responsibilities, and review functions assigned under the governing Data Transfer Agreement. |

Authorized users are responsible for using controlled data only for approved project activities, maintaining data in approved locations, preventing unauthorized access or redistribution, and protecting controlled information in analytical products.

This DMP will be reviewed at least annually and whenever there is a material change in:

- project personnel or access requirements;
- data types or sensitivity;
- storage systems;
- transfer mechanisms;
- publication or sharing plans;
- the governing agreement; or
- data-retention or disposition requirements.
